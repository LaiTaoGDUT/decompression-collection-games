Handmade needle-felt and wool miniature diorama, like a premium stop-motion film set photographed with soft natural light. Visible soft wool fibres, fuzzy edges, little running stitches, chunky rounded simplified shapes, gentle three-quarter view. NOT a clean 3D render, NOT flat vector, NOT photorealistic.
COLOUR: rich, harmonious, MULTI-HUE palette (several clearly different hues in every image, warm and cool together) with strong atmospheric perspective: farther = lighter, cooler, softer and hazier; nearer = warmer and more saturated. This is a BACKGROUND for a side-scrolling runner game: medium saturation, soft contrast, no hard black outlines, calm big shapes with breathing room.
