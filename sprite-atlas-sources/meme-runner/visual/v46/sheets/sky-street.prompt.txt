Game background SKY layer, 3:2 landscape, full-bleed opaque image. It is shown full-screen behind everything and never scrolls; the lower 40% will be hidden behind other scenery layers.
STYLE (shared by every background layer of this game):
Handmade needle-felt and wool miniature diorama, like a premium stop-motion film set photographed with soft natural light. Visible soft wool fibres, fuzzy edges, little running stitches, chunky rounded simplified shapes, gentle three-quarter view. NOT a clean 3D render, NOT flat vector, NOT photorealistic.
COLOUR: rich, harmonious, MULTI-HUE palette (several clearly different hues in every image, warm and cool together) with strong atmospheric perspective: farther = lighter, cooler, softer and hazier; nearer = warmer and more saturated. Absolutely NOT a monochrome or single-hue image, no overall colour filter.
This is a BACKGROUND for a side-scrolling runner game: medium saturation, soft contrast, no hard black outlines, calm big shapes with breathing room.
RESERVED FOR GAMEPLAY — never draw: red-and-yellow diagonal warning stripes, round shiny gold coins or golden discs, teal rings or pins, thin dark poles / lamp posts / fences / railings / benches / signposts, any text, letters, logos or signs, people or large animals.

SCENE "Blossom Hills — early morning":
Clear fresh morning sky: soft cornflower blue at the top, fading to pale aqua-cream, then a warm apricot-peach glow near the horizon. A soft pale-butter morning sun with a gentle halo in the upper-left quarter. Three or four fluffy felt cumulus clouds, cream-white with peach-lilac undersides, kept away from the top-centre (UI area). Horizon at about 60% of the height: a very distant range of soft periwinkle-lilac felt mountains in heavy morning haze, plus a band of mist. Below the horizon only soft hazy mint-green felt hills fading into mist (they will be covered).
